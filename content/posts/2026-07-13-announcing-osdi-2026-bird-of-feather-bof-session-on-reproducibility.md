<p><a href="https://repeto.cs.uchicago.edu/">The REPETO project</a> (an initiative funded by the <a href="https://www.nsf.gov/pubs/2022/nsf22553/nsf22553.htm">NSF FAIROS RCN</a> to build a network for practical reproducibility in experimental computer science) invites <a href="https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi26">OSDI '26</a> attendees to <strong>join our BoF session on 07/13/2026 to learn about packaging storage research experiments on <a href="https://www.chameleoncloud.org/">Chameleon</a></strong>. Chameleon is an open platform, which means that the hardware resources that folks use for their experiments will also be available to anybody who wishes to reproduce a presented experiment — all you will need is the packaging of the experiment itself!</p>

<p>We will teach the audience about tools and services Chameleon provides to share experiments, including the platform's integrated JupyterHub, <a href="https://www.chameleoncloud.org/blog/2022/01/24/interactive-science-made-easy-with-chameleon-daypass/">Chameleon daypass</a>, which allows you to give access to the testbed to anybody who wants to reproduce your experiment, and <a href="https://chameleoncloud.readthedocs.io/en/latest/technical/sharing.html">Chameleon Trovi</a>, a sharing portal that allows you to share digital research and education artifacts, such as packaged experiments, workshop tutorials, or class materials.</p>

<p>During the sessions:</p>

<ul>
	<li>The Chameleon team will present an overview of the testbed and highlight features that facilitate practical reproducibility in computer science research.</li>
	<li>Each session will include a panel discussion with <a href="https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi26">OSDI</a> authors who used Chameleon to package their experiments for the conference.</li>
	<li>The audience will have the opportunity to try replicating the experiments that the OSDI authors packaged on Chameleon hardware or try packaging their own experiments to share on Trovi.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Where/When?</h3>

<p>We will host <strong>one in-person session</strong> (one hour) at <a href="https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi26">OSDI '26</a> on <strong>July 13 (8pm-9pm)</strong>. Details about the BoF sessions will be available at the conference. Our agenda will include:</p>

<ul>
	<li>15 min Chameleon presentation</li>
	<li>10-15 min author demo</li>
	<li>15 min Q/A session</li>
</ul>
